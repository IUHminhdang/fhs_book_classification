7. `distilbert`: `distilbert-base-multilingual-cased` fine-tuning.
8. `mbert`: `bert-base-multilingual-cased` fine-tuning.
9. `phobert`: `vinai/phobert-base` fine-tuning.
10. `phobert_nli_vi`: `lizNguyen235/phobert-nli-vi` zero-shot classification.

For FastText experiments, use Vietnamese word segmentation with `underthesea` or a documented compatible segmenter before tokenization.
